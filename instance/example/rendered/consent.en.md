# Please read first: Agent Workspace is a prototype

- Agent Workspace is a proof of concept for testing, not a finished tool. It has not been approved by the Example.
- To evaluate the prototype, general chats are analysed by an AI and then read by the operator. Support profiles have separate terms; their chats are excluded unless optional evaluation is offered and you explicitly agree for that project.
- Do not enter any private or confidential data: no personal matters, no personnel data, no passwords or login details.
- Results can be wrong. Check them before you use them.
- Anyone who misuses the tool will be held responsible.
